I'm a senior at Dartmouth studying Computer Science. I grew up in Palo Alto, CA. My goal is to work on the frontier of systems and applied AI.

In addition to my coursework at Dartmouth, I work in the LISP Lab with Professor Peter Chin and the Breuer Lab with Professor Adam Breuer. I've worked on federated learning privacy exploits, data compression for defending against adversarial examples, and finding ways to mechanistically measure and edit personality of Language Models.

I'm an avid fan of science-fiction and biographies. Some personal favorites include the Three Body Problem, by Cixin Liu, Foundation, by Isaac Asimov, and nearly anything written by Ted Chiang or Walter Isaacson.
